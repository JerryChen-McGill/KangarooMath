Answer: B — 1090


Solution
Subtract from left to right: 1999 − 999 = 1000; 1000 − 99 = 901; 901 − 9 = 892. Important: the PDF answer key says B (1090), but the expression as printed evaluates to 892; none of the choices matches. This is a source inconsistency to point out in class.
