## Q1 · 图形补全
Which small square completes the kangaroo competition symbol?
OPTIONS: A. Tile A | B. Tile B | C. Tile C | D. Tile D | E. Tile E
ANSWER: B
HINTS:
- 先标出缺格边缘的接点。
- 同时检查上下左右。
- B能使轮廓连续。
SOLUTION:
The missing tile must continue every contour where it meets the neighboring tiles. B matches the kangaroo's neck boundary and the short contour entering at the top; the other tiles introduce broken or misplaced lines.
OTHER METHODS:
- Trace boundary entry points before considering the inside of a tile.
TEACHING TIP:
Keep each choice in its printed orientation unless a rotation is explicitly allowed.
EXTENSION:
After inserting B, rotate the entire completed picture90°. Does the inserted tile still fit?
EXTENSION ANSWER:
Yes. Rotating the entire picture preserves all boundary matches.
EXTENSION SOLUTION:
Yes. Rotating the entire picture preserves all boundary matches.

## Q2 · 连续加减
Simplify2+2−2+2−2+2−2+2−2+2.
OPTIONS: A. 0 | B. 2 | C. 4 | D. 12 | E. 20
ANSWER: C
HINTS:
- 同级运算从左到右。
- 可配成加2减2的抵消对。
- 最后留下两个正2。
SOLUTION:
There are six positive2s and four negative2s. The result is12−8=4. Or cancel four(+2−2) pairs, leaving2+2.
OTHER METHODS:
- Calculate a running total:2,4,2,4,2,4,2,4,2,4.
TEACHING TIP:
Keep the initial positive term when counting signs.
EXTENSION:
Append−2+2 to the expression. What is the result?
EXTENSION ANSWER:
4
EXTENSION SOLUTION:
4; the added pair has net change0.

## Q3 · 加法
Andrzej receives3 toy cars,4 balls,3 teddy bears,10 pens,2 chocolate bars and1 book. How many items altogether?
OPTIONS: A. 15 | B. 17 | C. 20 | D. 23 | E. 27
ANSWER: D
HINTS:
- 逐类记录数量。
- 别漏掉一本书。
- 凑十再相加。
SOLUTION:
Add all categories:3+4+3+10+2+1=23. Grouping(3+3+4)+10+(2+1)=10+10+3 helps.
OTHER METHODS:
- Use a category-and-count table.
TEACHING TIP:
Distinguish the number of types of gifts from the number of individual items.
EXTENSION:
He receives2 additional books. How many items now?
EXTENSION ANSWER:
25
EXTENSION SOLUTION:
25;23+2=25.

## Q4 · 分割图形
Which piece is absent from the divided square?
OPTIONS: A. Piece A | B. Piece B | C. Piece C | D. Piece D | E. Piece E
ANSWER: D
HINTS:
- 先找所有独立区域。
- 旋转后比形状与比例。
- 图中没有U形的一块。
SOLUTION:
Trace the actual region boundaries. A is a square, B a rectangle, and the two differently proportioned L-shaped pieces match C and E after rotation. No region has D's U-shaped outline.
OTHER METHODS:
- Cut out or color the five regions and compare them one at a time.
TEACHING TIP:
Do not confuse a union of several regions with one original piece.
EXTENSION:
Rotate the whole divided square180°. Which option is still absent?
EXTENSION ANSWER:
D
EXTENSION SOLUTION:
D; rotating the whole picture does not change which shapes occur.

## Q5 · 逻辑对应
Gina, Lena, Suzie and Joanna have birthdays on March1, May17, July20 and March20. Lena and Suzie share a month; Gina and Suzie share a day number. Who was born May17?
OPTIONS: A. Gina | B. Lena | C. Suzie | D. Joanna | E. Cannot be determined
ANSWER: D
HINTS:
- 先找重复月份。
- 再找重复日号20。
- 用排除法安排剩余生日。
SOLUTION:
The repeated month is March, so Lena and Suzie take March1 and March20. The only repeated day number is20, so Suzie=March20, Gina=July20 and Lena=March1. Joanna is left with May17.
OTHER METHODS:
- Build a four-person matching grid and cross out impossible dates.
TEACHING TIP:
A day of the month means the numeral, not a weekday.
EXTENSION:
Change only May17 to May18. Who has that birthday?
EXTENSION ANSWER:
Joanna
EXTENSION SOLUTION:
Joanna; the two repeated-month/day deductions are unchanged.

## Q6 · 单位换算
A heart averages70 beats per minute. About how many beats per hour?
OPTIONS: A. 42000 | B. 7000 | C. 4200 | D. 700 | E. 420
ANSWER: C
HINTS:
- 一小时有60分钟。
- 每分钟70次。
- 70乘60。
SOLUTION:
One hour contains60 minutes. At70 beats per minute,70×60=4200 beats.
OTHER METHODS:
- Calculate7×6=42 and restore the two zero factors.
TEACHING TIP:
Write units so minutes cancel; this is the problem's assumed average.
EXTENSION:
At72 beats per minute, how many beats in one hour?
EXTENSION ANSWER:
4320
EXTENSION SOLUTION:
4320;72×60=4320.

## Q7 · 周长差
ABCD is a square of side10 cm. ATMD is a rectangle with short side3 cm and long sideAD=10 cm. What is the difference between their perimeters?
OPTIONS: A. 14 cm | B. 10 cm | C. 7 cm | D. 6 cm | E. 4 cm
ANSWER: A
HINTS:
- 矩形共享AD这条10厘米长边。
- 分别求40和26。
- 周长差14。
SOLUTION:
Square perimeter=4×10=40 cm. Rectangle perimeter=2×(10+3)=26 cm. Difference=14 cm. Two equal10 cm sides cancel, leaving2×(10−3).
OTHER METHODS:
- Compare only the two differing sides:2×(10−3).
TEACHING TIP:
The prompt omits a drawing here; use the shared sideAD to identify the rectangle's long side.
EXTENSION:
The rectangle's short side is4 cm instead. Find the perimeter difference.
EXTENSION ANSWER:
12 cm
EXTENSION SOLUTION:
12 cm;40−2×14=12.

## Q8 · 折纸
Which outline cannot be made by folding a rectangle just once?
OPTIONS: A. Figure A | B. Figure B | C. Figure C | D. Figure D | E. Figure E
ANSWER: B
HINTS:
- 一次折叠只有一条直折痕。
- 沿折痕反射折过去的部分。
- 检查B的凹口和尖角能否同时形成。
SOLUTION:
A single straight crease reflects one connected part of the sheet onto the other. Trace the reflected rectangle boundaries: A,C,D,E can be realized by suitable folds, whereas B's inner notch and diagonal outer tip cannot be produced together by one fold.
OTHER METHODS:
- Use paper trials or transparent overlays for all candidate outlines.
TEACHING TIP:
Evaluate the full visible union of the folded layers, not only the folded flap. 4-Point Questions
EXTENSION:
Fold a rectangle exactly in half along a line parallel to a side. What shape is its outside boundary?
EXTENSION ANSWER:
A rectangle with one dimension halved.
EXTENSION SOLUTION:
A rectangle with one dimension halved.

## Q9 · 数字出现次数
Houses are numbered1 through24. How many times does digit2 occur?
OPTIONS: A. 2 | B. 4 | C. 8 | D. 16 | E. 32
ANSWER: C
HINTS:
- 按个位和十位分开数。
- 个位有2、12、22。
- 十位有20到24，共5次。
SOLUTION:
Units-position2 occurs in2,12,22:3 times. Tens-position2 occurs in20,21,22,23,24:5 times. Total8;22 contributes twice.
OTHER METHODS:
- List2,12,20,21,22,23,24 and count their individual2s.
TEACHING TIP:
Number of labels containing2 is different from number of digit occurrences.
EXTENSION:
For houses1 through29, how many times does digit2 occur?
EXTENSION ANSWER:
13
EXTENSION SOLUTION:
13;3 in the units place plus10 in the tens place.

## Q10 · 天平
Six identical oranges balance two identical melons after one melon is added to the orange side. How many oranges weigh as much as one melon?
OPTIONS: A. 2 | B. 3 | C. 4 | D. 5 | E. 6
ANSWER: E
HINTS:
- 看加完后的平衡状态。
- 两边同时拿走一个瓜。
- 剩6个橙子等于1个瓜。
SOLUTION:
The final balance means6 oranges+1 melon=2 melons. Remove one melon from each side:6 oranges=1 melon.
OTHER METHODS:
- Write6o+m=2m and subtract m.
TEACHING TIP:
Do not treat the initially tilted scale as already balanced.
EXTENSION:
Eight oranges plus one melon balance three melons. How many oranges equal one melon?
EXTENSION ANSWER:
4
EXTENSION SOLUTION:
4;8 oranges=2 melons.

## Q11 · 线条匹配
Which line is not part of the castle sketch?
OPTIONS: A. Line A | B. Line B | C. Line C | D. Line D | E. Line E
ANSWER: C
HINTS:
- 沿候选线从一端走到另一端。
- 转折次序也必须相同。
- C的双尖峰凹口组合不在原图。
SOLUTION:
Follow each candidate through the castle outline. A matches the stepped section; B and D match sloping and vertical corners; E matches the double curved tower top. C's two roof peaks separated by a deep rectangular notch do not appear as one continuous part.
OTHER METHODS:
- Use transparent tracing paper to slide each line over the sketch.
TEACHING TIP:
A partial resemblance at one corner is insufficient; match the whole connected segment.
EXTENSION:
Trace the castle's double curved tower top. Which original choice matches it?
EXTENSION ANSWER:
E.
EXTENSION SOLUTION:
E.

## Q12 · 最值常识
Add17 to the smallest two-digit number, then divide by the largest one-digit number. What is the result?
OPTIONS: A. 3 | B. 6 | C. 9 | D. 11 | E. 27
ANSWER: A
HINTS:
- 先确定10和9。
- 先加17得到27。
- 再除以9。
SOLUTION:
The smallest two-digit positive integer is10 and the largest one-digit integer is9. Compute(10+17)÷9=27÷9=3.
OTHER METHODS:
- Write the entire calculation with parentheses before evaluating.
TEACHING TIP:
Use base10 positive integers; keep the addition inside the division's numerator.
EXTENSION:
Add26 to the smallest two-digit number, then divide by9. What is the result?
EXTENSION ANSWER:
4
EXTENSION SOLUTION:
4;(10+26)÷9=4.

## Q13 · 符号计数
Ancient symbols represent1,10 and60. Which notation represents124?
OPTIONS: A. Notation A | B. Notation B | C. Notation C | D. Notation D | E. Notation E
ANSWER: E
HINTS:
- 先用最大的60为单位。
- 124减120余4。
- 找两个60符号加四个1符号。
SOLUTION:
Decompose124=2×60+0×10+4×1. E has two60-symbols and four1-symbols. There is no10-symbol.
OTHER METHODS:
- Translate each option into a numeric sum.
TEACHING TIP:
Read the legend rather than inferring symbol value from size alone.
EXTENSION:
Write134 using the same symbols with the fewest symbols.
EXTENSION ANSWER:
Two60-symbols, one10-symbol and four1-symbols:120+10+4=134.
EXTENSION SOLUTION:
Two60-symbols, one10-symbol and four1-symbols:120+10+4=134.

## Q14 · 时钟分组
Which clock partition has four consecutive sums of the numbers in its parts?
OPTIONS: A. Partition A | B. Partition B | C. Partition C | D. Partition D | E. Partition E
ANSWER: B
HINTS:
- 先算1到12的总和78。
- 四个连续和必须18、19、20、21。
- 逐区核对B的分组。
SOLUTION:
The clock numbers sum1+…+12=78. If four consecutive sums are n,n+1,n+2,n+3, then4n+6=78, so they must be18,19,20,21. B groups(12,1,2,3),(9,10),(11,4,5),(6,7,8), giving18,19,20,21.
OTHER METHODS:
- Test each partition against the forced set18–21.
TEACHING TIP:
A curved region can contain separated labels around the rim; follow the actual boundary.
EXTENSION:
What four consecutive integers have sum86?
EXTENSION ANSWER:
20,21,22,23, since4n+6=86.
EXTENSION SOLUTION:
20,21,22,23, since4n+6=86.

## Q15 · 周长分配
Klara and Zoe have60 matches. Klara makes a triangle with6 matches per side. Zoe makes a rectangle from the rest with one side6 matches long. How many matches form each longer side?
OPTIONS: A. 9 | B. 12 | C. 15 | D. 18 | E. 30
ANSWER: C
HINTS:
- 三角形先用18根。
- 剩42是矩形周长。
- 减两条短边再除以2。
SOLUTION:
The triangle uses3×6=18, leaving42. The rectangle's two short sides use12, leaving30 for two long sides. Each long side uses15.
OTHER METHODS:
- Use rectangle semiperimeter42÷2=21 and subtract6.
TEACHING TIP:
A polygon side length in matches is not its full perimeter.
EXTENSION:
Start with66 matches and keep both given side lengths6. Find each long side.
EXTENSION ANSWER:
18
EXTENSION SOLUTION:
18;(66−18−12)÷2=18.

## Q16 · 路径长度
Miki, Niki and Oki jump at the same speed along the grid paths shown. Which statement is true?
OPTIONS: A. Miki and Oki finish together | B. Niki finishes first | C. Oki finishes last | D. All finish together | E. Miki and Niki finish together
ANSWER: E
HINTS:
- 把每段折线拆成单位格边。
- 横向也要计入路程。
- Miki和Niki等长、Oki较短。
SOLUTION:
Count unit grid edges: Miki's path has9 vertical and9 horizontal units, total18; Niki's has9 vertical and9 horizontal units, total18; Oki's has9 vertical and8 horizontal units, total17. With equal speeds Oki finishes first, and Miki and Niki finish together. Therefore E alone is true.
OTHER METHODS:
- Lay string along each path, straighten it, and compare.
TEACHING TIP:
Use equal-speed comparison only after counting all segments; do not compare just the picture heights. 5-Point Questions
EXTENSION:
Miki and Niki keep their equal path lengths, but Niki moves twice as fast. Who finishes first between them?
EXTENSION ANSWER:
Niki
EXTENSION SOLUTION:
Niki; time=distance÷speed, so Niki takes half Miki's time.

## Q17 · 逻辑排除
Mike, Nate, Oliver and Paul each own one different pet: cat, dog, goldfish, canary. Nate's has fur; Oliver's has four legs; Paul has a bird; Mike and Nate dislike cats. Which statement is NOT true?
OPTIONS: A. Oliver has a dog | B. Paul has a canary | C. Mike has a goldfish | D. Oliver has a cat | E. Nate has a dog
ANSWER: A
HINTS:
- 先确定Paul的鸟。
- Nate有毛且不是猫，所以是狗。
- Oliver是猫，Mike是鱼。
SOLUTION:
Paul=canary. Nate's furry pet is not a cat, so Nate=dog. Oliver's four-legged pet must then be cat. Mike=goldfish. Thus A is false and B–E are true.
OTHER METHODS:
- Make a person/pet elimination grid.
TEACHING TIP:
Underline NOT so students choose the false statement after solving the assignment.
EXTENSION:
Using the same clues, who has the dog?
EXTENSION ANSWER:
Nate.
EXTENSION SOLUTION:
Nate.

## Q18 · 时间差
Mary leaves6:55 and arrives7:32. Zoe needs12 minutes less and arrives7:45. When did Zoe leave?
OPTIONS: A. 7:07 | B. 7:20 | C. 7:25 | D. 7:30 | E. 7:33
ANSWER: B
HINTS:
- 跨整点计算Mary的37分钟。
- Zoe少12分钟，需25分钟。
- 从到校时刻倒减25分钟。
SOLUTION:
Mary travels37 minutes:5 to7:00 and32 more. Zoe takes37−12=25 minutes. Her departure is7:45−25 minutes=7:20.
OTHER METHODS:
- Use a timeline with departure, journey duration and arrival.
TEACHING TIP:
Separate a duration from a clock reading; do not subtract12 directly from the arrival time.
EXTENSION:
Zoe arrives8:00 with the same journey time. When did she leave?
EXTENSION ANSWER:
7:35
EXTENSION SOLUTION:
7:35;8:00−25 minutes=7:35.

## Q19 · 立方体
Robert uses half his cubes for a tunnel and some of the rest for a stepped pyramid. How many cubes remain?
OPTIONS: A. 34 | B. 28 | C. 22 | D. 18 | E. 15
ANSWER: D
HINTS:
- 隧道用外体减去贯通的洞。
- 一半用64则另一半也64。
- 金字塔按25、9、9、1、1、1分层。
SOLUTION:
The tunnel has a5×5 cross-section with a3×3 opening and depth4: (25−9)×4=64 cubes. Since this is half, another64 remain. The pyramid has a5×5 base layer, two3×3 layers and three1×1 layers:25+18+3=46. Leftover=64−46=18.
OTHER METHODS:
- Count tunnel layers as four identical frames of16 cubes.
TEACHING TIP:
Hidden cubes matter; the opening runs through the full depth, and the pyramid contains repeated layers.
EXTENSION:
Keep the same tunnel but remove the pyramid's topmost cube before building it. How many cubes remain?
EXTENSION ANSWER:
19
EXTENSION SOLUTION:
19; the pyramid then uses45, so64−45=19.

## Q20 · 年龄倍数
A daughter is3 years old; her mother is28 years older. How many years until the mother is three times the daughter's age?
OPTIONS: A. 9 | B. 12 | C. 10 | D. 1 | E. 11
ANSWER: E
HINTS:
- 年龄差始终28。
- 三倍关系的差是女儿年龄的2倍。
- 女儿14岁，还需11年。
SOLUTION:
The mother is31 now. Their difference stays28. At a3:1 age ratio the difference is two daughter-age units, so the daughter must be14. Wait14−3=11 years; the mother is then42.
OTHER METHODS:
- Solve31+t=3(3+t).
TEACHING TIP:
Both people age by the same amount; do not hold the mother's age fixed.
EXTENSION:
The daughter is5 with the same28-year gap. How long until the3:1 ratio?
EXTENSION ANSWER:
9 years
EXTENSION SOLUTION:
9 years; she must still reach14.

## Q21 · 组合
Choose a trio: one of2 violinists, one of2 pianists and one of2 drummers. How many trios must be tried to test every possibility?
OPTIONS: A. 3 | B. 4 | C. 8 | D. 24 | E. 25
ANSWER: C
HINTS:
- 按乐器分三步选择。
- 每一步有2种。
- 画三层树状图。
SOLUTION:
Each violinist can pair with either pianist and either drummer. Independent choices give2×2×2=8 trios.
OTHER METHODS:
- List binary labels111,112,121,122,211,212,221,222.
TEACHING TIP:
Roles are distinct; do not multiply by3! for ordering the same trio.
EXTENSION:
There are3 violinists,2 pianists and2 drummers. How many trios?
EXTENSION ANSWER:
12
EXTENSION SOLUTION:
12;3×2×2=12.

## Q22 · 废料再利用
One medal is cut from each square plate. Scraps from4 used plates make1 new plate. Starting with16 plates, what is the maximum number of medals?
OPTIONS: A. 17 | B. 19 | C. 20 | D. 21 | E. 32
ANSWER: D
HINTS:
- 第一轮16枚。
- 16份废料可换4块板。
- 4枚的废料再换1块板。
SOLUTION:
First make16 medals, whose scraps make4 plates. These give4 more medals; their scraps make1 plate, giving1 last medal. Total16+4+1=21. One scrap-unit remains, insufficient for another plate.
OTHER METHODS:
- Track whole plates and scrap-units separately; each recycling medal consumes a net3 scrap-units.
TEACHING TIP:
Include scraps generated from recycled plates, but do not borrow future material.
EXTENSION:
Start with20 plates under the same rule. What is the maximum?
EXTENSION ANSWER:
26
EXTENSION SOLUTION:
26;20 medals, then5, then1. After the last round2 scrap-units remain.

## Q23 · 排名
28 students finish with different scores. The number above Tom is half the number below him. What is his rank?
OPTIONS: A. 6th | B. 7th | C. 8th | D. 9th | E. 10th
ANSWER: E
HINTS:
- 总人数减去Tom。
- 前后人数比1:2。
- 前9人，所以第10。
SOLUTION:
Exclude Tom:27 students. Split them in ratio1:2, so9 are above and18 below. Rank=9+1=10.
OTHER METHODS:
- Solve a+2a=27 and then add one.
TEACHING TIP:
Scores differ, so ranks have no ties.
EXTENSION:
There are31 students with the same ratio. What is Tom's rank?
EXTENSION ANSWER:
11th
EXTENSION SOLUTION:
11th;30÷3=10 above.

## Q24 · 数位
An odometer reads187569, with all digits different. After how many more kilometers will it next show all distinct digits?
OPTIONS: A. 777 | B. 12431 | C. 431 | D. 21 | E. 11
ANSWER: D
HINTS:
- 先看个位进位后的前缀。
- 18757x和18758x都已有重复。
- 187590首次全部不同。
SOLUTION:
187570–187579 all repeat7 in the fixed prefix18757. 187580–187589 repeat8 in prefix18758. The next reading187590 has digits1,8,7,5,9,0 all distinct. Difference187590−187569=21. No smaller positive increment works.
OTHER METHODS:
- Scan short blocks sharing a repeated prefix rather than checking every number.
TEACHING TIP:
All-different is a condition on every digit, and the current valid reading is excluded by 'again after traveling'.
EXTENSION:
Starting from187590, after how many kilometers is the next all-distinct reading?
EXTENSION ANSWER:
2
EXTENSION SOLUTION:
2;187591 repeats1, while187592 has six different digits.
