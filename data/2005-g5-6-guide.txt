2005 · Grade 5–6 · Q2 · 3 分 · 和差问题 糖果
Together, Anna and Olla have ten pieces of candy. Olla has two more than Anna. How many does Olla have?
Correct Answer: C — 6

延申拓展
Together they have 18 candies, and Olla has four more. How many does Olla have?
小改动：总数10改成18，差2改成4。

同类方法提示
Subtract the difference, divide the remainder equally, and return the excess to the larger group.

拓展答案与解析
11: (18 − 4) ÷ 2 + 4 = 11.

全部 Hint
1. 先找出两人相差多少颗。
2. 去掉多出来的2颗，剩下平均分。
3. 每人先分4颗，再给Olla补回2颗。

详细解答
Remove Olla's extra two candies. The remaining eight split evenly, four each. Give the extra two back to Olla: 4 + 2 = 6.

其他思路
Draw a two-bar model, with one bar two units longer.

备课提示
Invite students to explain the approach and check the original problem statement.

2005 · Grade 5–6 · Q4 · 3 分 · 分类 计数 动物腿数
Eva lives with her parents, a brother, a dog, two cats, two parrots, and four goldfish. How many legs do they have altogether?
Correct Answer: D — 24

延申拓展
What if Eva's family also adopted one extra dog? How many legs would there be?
小改动：只增加一条狗，其余数量不变。

同类方法提示
Add the new dog's four legs to the original sum.

拓展答案与解析
28 legs: 24 + 4 = 28.

全部 Hint
1. 先数清楚一共有几个人。
2. 分别计算两足和四足动物的腿。
3. 金鱼没有腿，不要算进去。

详细解答
Four humans have 8 legs; the dog has 4, the cats have 8, the parrots have 4, and the fish have none. Total: 8 + 4 + 8 + 4 = 24.

其他思路
Make a species-by-species counting table.

备课提示
Invite students to explain the approach and check the original problem statement.

2005 · Grade 5–6 · Q5 · 3 分 · 乘法加法 位值
Evaluate 2005 × 100 + 2005.
Correct Answer: D — 202505

延申拓展
Evaluate 2005 × 10 + 2005.
小改动：把乘数100改为10。

同类方法提示
Factor the common 2005 or calculate the product before adding.

拓展答案与解析
22055: 20050 + 2005 = 22055.

全部 Hint
1. 先算乘法部分。
2. 2005乘100相当于末尾添两个0。
3. 再将2005加到200500。

详细解答
Multiplying by 100 gives 200500. Adding 2005 gives 202505, which corresponds to answer D.

其他思路
Factor the original expression: 2005 × (100 + 1).

2005 · Grade 5–6 · Q7 · 3 分 · 分数 余量 分类
Of 24 marbles, one-eighth are white, and two-thirds of the remaining marbles are red. How many are brown?
Correct Answer: D — 7

延申拓展
There are 48 marbles with the same color fractions. How many are brown?
小改动：只把总数24增加到48。

同类方法提示
Apply the one-eighth fraction first, then two-thirds to the remainder.

拓展答案与解析
14: white 6, remainder 42, red 28, brown 14.

全部 Hint
1. 先求白色弹珠数。
2. 红色是剩下弹珠的三分之二，不是原来24颗的三分之二。
3. 最后从21里减去14。

详细解答
White = 24 ÷ 8 = 3. Remaining = 21. Red = (2/3) × 21 = 14. Brown = 21 − 14 = 7.

其他思路
Use a fraction bar for the 21 marbles left after removing white ones.

备课提示
Invite students to explain the approach and check the original problem statement.

2005 · Grade 5–6 · Q9 · 3 分 · 数论 整除规则
Which of 987, 444, 204, 105, and 103 cannot be three times a natural number?
Correct Answer: E — 103

延申拓展
Which of 126, 213, 302 and 414 is not divisible by three?
小改动：只替换各候选数，仍检验能否被3整除。

同类方法提示
Check whether the digit sum is divisible by three.

拓展答案与解析
302: 3+0+2=5, which is not a multiple of three.

全部 Hint
1. 回忆3的整除规则。
2. 分别计算每个选项的数字和。
3. 103的数字和是4。

详细解答
A number is divisible by three exactly when its digit sum is divisible by three. The digit sums are 24, 12, 6, 6, and 4 respectively. Only 103 fails.

其他思路
Divide directly by 3 to check remainders.

2005 · Grade 5–6 · Q10 · 3 分 · 分数 顺序运算
How many hours are half of one-third of one-quarter of 24 hours?
Correct Answer: C — 1

延申拓展
Find half of one-third of one-quarter of 48 hours.
小改动：只把24小时改为48小时。

同类方法提示
Divide successively by 4, 3, then 2.

拓展答案与解析
2 hours: 48 ÷ 4 ÷ 3 ÷ 2 = 2.

全部 Hint
1. 先求24的四分之一。
2. 再求6的三分之一。
3. 最后求2的一半。

详细解答
A quarter of 24 hours is 6 hours; a third of 6 is 2 hours; half of 2 is 1 hour.

其他思路
Compute 24 × 1/4 × 1/3 × 1/2 in one expression.

2005 · Grade 5–6 · Q11 · 4 分 · 剪切 数列 增量
A napkin is cut into 10 pieces, and one piece is cut into 10 pieces three more times. How many pieces are there at the end?
Correct Answer: C — 37

延申拓展
Start with eight pieces; twice cut one existing piece into eight. How many pieces are there?
小改动：将每次剪成10块改为8块，并只追加两次。

同类方法提示
Each subsequent cut adds seven, because one existing piece is replaced.

拓展答案与解析
22: 8 + 2 × 7 = 22.

全部 Hint
1. 第一次剪完有10块。
2. 后面每把1块剪成10块，净增加9块。
3. 一共追加三次这样的操作。

详细解答
After the first cut there are 10 pieces. Each subsequent operation replaces one piece with ten, adding a net nine. Three additional operations give 10 + 3 × 9 = 37.

其他思路
Write the sequence 10, 19, 28, 37.

Invite students to explain the approach and check the original problem statement.

2005 · Grade 5–6 · Q12 · 4 分 · 往返 速度时间
Mowgli walks to the beach and rides an elephant back in 40 minutes. An elephant round trip takes 32 minutes. How long does walking both ways take?
Correct Answer: D — 48 minutes

延申拓展
A walk-and-cycle round trip takes 50 minutes, while cycling both ways takes 30 minutes. How long would walking both ways take?
小改动：把大象换成骑车，同时替换两个时间。

同类方法提示
First find the cycling time one way, then the walking time one way.

拓展答案与解析
70 minutes: cycling one way 15, walking one way 35, double gives 70.

全部 Hint
1. 先求坐大象单程所需时间。
2. 从40分钟中减掉大象单程。
3. 步行单程24分钟，往返要乘2。

详细解答
One elephant trip takes 32 ÷ 2 = 16 minutes. One walking trip takes 40 − 16 = 24 minutes. Two walking trips take 2 × 24 = 48 minutes.

其他思路
Use equations w + e = 40 and 2e = 32.

Invite students to explain the approach and check the original problem statement.

2005 · Grade 5–6 · Q14 · 4 分 · 平均数 分配 对比
Peanuts are assigned to ten people: one gets 5 kg, two get 4 kg, four get 2 kg, two get 1.5 kg, and one gets nothing. How many would receive more under equal sharing?
Correct Answer: E — 7

延申拓展
If the same ten people shared 30 kg equally, how many of the original recipients would receive more than in the original plan?
小改动：把平均分配时的总重量从24改成30，保持原来的分配对照。

同类方法提示
Find the new average and count those originally below it.

拓展答案与解析
7 people: equal share is 3 kg, exceeding the original shares of 2, 1.5, and 0.

全部 Hint
1. 先计算花生总重量。
2. 再除以总人数，求平均值。
3. 数出原来少于2.4千克的人。

详细解答
Total = 5 + 2×4 + 4×2 + 2×1.5 = 24 kg. Equal sharing among ten gives 2.4 kg per person. All four at 2 kg, both at 1.5 kg, and the one with none gain: 4 + 2 + 1 = 7.

其他思路
Organize the original amounts into a frequency table.

备课提示
Invite students to explain the approach and check the original problem statement.

2005 · Grade 5–6 · Q15 · 4 分 · 排列组合 奇数
How many two-digit numbers can be formed using two different odd digits?
Correct Answer: B — 20

延申拓展
How many three-digit numbers have three different odd digits?
小改动：把两位数改成三位数。

同类方法提示
Multiply the choices for hundreds, tens, and units.

拓展答案与解析
60: 5 × 4 × 3 = 60.

全部 Hint
1. 可选的奇数数字有几个？
2. 先选十位，再选个位。
3. 个位不能与十位相同。

详细解答
The odd digits are 1, 3, 5, 7, 9. Choose the tens digit in five ways and a different units digit in four ways: 5 × 4 = 20.

其他思路
Make a five-row listing by tens digit.

备课提示
Invite students to explain the approach and check the original problem statement.
