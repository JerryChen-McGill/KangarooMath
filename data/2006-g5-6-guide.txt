2006 · Grade 5–6 · Q1 · 3 分 · 代数 逆运算
3 × 2006 = 2005 + 2007 + a. Find a.
Correct Answer: D ✅

延申拓展
3 × 2010 = 2009 + 2011 + a. Find a.

小改动：只替换年份数字，保留对称加法关系。

同类方法提示
The neighboring years add to twice the middle year.

拓展答案与解析
2010: 3×2010−(2009+2011)=2010.

全部 Hint
1. 先算左边总和。
2. 把已知两个加数相加。
3. 用总和减去已知部分。

详细解答
Subtract 2005+2007=4012 from 3×2006=6018. Thus a=2006.

其他思路
Use the symmetry 2005+2007=2×2006.

2006 · Grade 5–6 · Q28 · 5 分 · 分数 班级人数 整数
In a class, 1/8 get C, 1/6 get B and 2/3 get A; fewer than 30 students, none get D. How many get F?
Correct Answer: B ✅

延申拓展
For a class of fewer than 50, 1/6 get C, 1/4 get B and 1/2 get A; the total is the least positive valid class size. How many receive F?

小改动：更换分数及人数上限，仍用公倍数求总人数。

同类方法提示
LCM of denominators 6,4,2 is 12; subtract counts.

拓展答案与解析
1 student: 12−(2+3+6)=1.

全部 Hint
1. 分数对应的人数必须是整数。
2. 找8、6、3的公倍数且小于30。
3. 24人中A、B、C共23人。

详细解答
The class size must be divisible by 8, 6 and 3 for the fractions to give whole students, so it is 24 (less than 30). A+B+C share = 2/3+1/6+1/8=16/24+4/24+3/24=23/24. One student gets F.

其他思路
Use the least common multiple and a common denominator.

2006 · Grade 5–6 · Q30 · 5 分 · 集合 整除 恰好两次
Numbers 1–2006 are underlined by three people for divisibility by 2, 3 and 4 respectively. How many are underlined exactly twice?
Correct Answer: C ✅

延申拓展
Among integers 1–120, count those divisible by exactly two of 2,3,4.

小改动：把上限2006改成120。

同类方法提示
Count (multiples of 4 or 6) but remove multiples of 12 twice.

拓展答案与解析
30: (30−10)+(20−10)=20+10=30.

全部 Hint
1. 能被4整除一定也能被2整除。
2. 分类：4的倍数但非3的倍数，或6的倍数但非4的倍数。
3. 分别数两类，不能重复计算12的倍数。

详细解答
A number divisible by 4 is automatically divisible by 2. Exactly two underline marks occur for multiples of 4 not divisible by 3, and for multiples of 6 not divisible by 4. Count multiples of 4: floor(2006/4)=501; subtract multiples of 12: floor(2006/12)=167 ⇒334. Count multiples of 6: floor(2006/6)=334; subtract multiples of 12:167 ⇒167. Total 334+167=501 (answer C).

其他思路
Use a Venn diagram and inclusion-exclusion.

Invite students to justify the method; for illustrated questions, compare their reasoning with the diagram in the original PDF.
